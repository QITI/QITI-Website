Our paper on preparing steady-states of systems with non-Markovian bath interactions is [now available](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19286) on the Arxiv! This is an experimental work from the QSim project that explores how structured interactions (non-Markovianity) between a quantum system and its environment can lead to averaged behavior (steady-states) which are disallowed for analogous idealized (Markovian) systems.
