Bloodstone is the flagship ytterbium-ion trapped-ion quantum processor effort at QITI. The project focuses on scalable hardware, reliable operations, and a control and software stack that supports both quantum simulation and quantum computation.

## What Bloodstone is for
- Scaling programmable trapped-ion systems for quantum simulation and quantum information processing
- Enabling high-fidelity control, flexible connectivity, and experiment automation needed for larger systems
- Providing a platform that connects hardware development to algorithms, benchmarking, and applications

## What makes it distinctive
- Engineering emphasis on stability, uptime, and repeatable experimental workflows
- Design choices aimed at scaling and modular upgrades
- Close integration with open tooling and community efforts through Open Quantum Design (OQD)
