At the core of the Bloodstone processor is a segmented blade electrode ion trap apparatus housed in a room temperature apparatus with pressure at the boundary of Extreme High Vacuum (<1.5 x 10<sup>-12</sup> mbar measured by an ion gauge). The Bloodstone processor is engineered following systems design principles, to be modular and robust againt drifts. The optical architecture includes individual qubit addressing from two sides, using a mirrored Acousto-Optic Deflector set up, and an in situ Mid-circuit measurement and reset based on holographic optical engineering.
