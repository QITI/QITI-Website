We welcome talented and motivated researchers who are excited about building and using trapped-ion quantum systems. QITI is based at the Institute for Quantum Computing (IQC) and the Department of Physics and Astronomy at the University of Waterloo.

## Who we are looking for
We regularly host:
- Undergraduate researchers and co-op students
- Graduate students (MSc and PhD)
- Postdoctoral fellows
- Visiting researchers and collaborators

Strong preparation in one or more of the following is helpful:
- Atomic, molecular and optical physics
- Quantum information, quantum control, and quantum many-body physics
- Experimental physics and instrumentation (optics, lasers, electronics, RF, vacuum)
- Software for experimental control, compilers, simulation, and data analysis

## Current directions
Examples of active areas include:
- Scalable trapped-ion quantum processor development (including the Bloodstone system)
- Quantum simulation experiments and algorithms
- Open full-stack control and tooling through Open Quantum Design (OQD)

## How to apply
Please email Prof. K. Rajibul Islam with:
- A short statement of interests (what you want to work on and why)
- A CV
- For graduate applicants: an unofficial transcript is helpful
- For postdoctoral applicants: a short research statement and two to three references

If you are responding to a specific opportunity, mention it in the subject line.

## Collaborations
We are interested in collaborations across quantum science and engineering. If you have a project idea that could benefit from programmable trapped-ion platforms or open full-stack tooling, please reach out.
