Quantum simulation and computation

We develop quantum control protocols for trapped-ion qubits and explore algorithms for quantum simulation of complex, interacting many-body systems.

Flagship platforms

Bloodstone (Yb)

Our flagship ytterbium trapped-ion system aimed at scaling programmable quantum simulation and computation. Track milestones, capabilities, and current directions.

Bloodstone page

QuantumION (Ba)

A barium-ion platform with attractive optical and qubit properties, complementing our ytterbium system and enabling new experimental opportunities.
